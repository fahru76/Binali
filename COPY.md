**Scope:** Copy only. Claims are bounded by the supplied photos, SSM extract and two briefs. Anything marked **[REVIEW]** requires Binali's confirmation before publication.

## 1. Hero options

### Option A — recommended
**BM:** Rumah setingkat moden, dibina untuk kehidupan anda.
**EN:** A modern single-storey home, built for your life.

**Rationale:** The photos repeatedly show completed single-storey residential homes with contemporary façades, porches, garages and finished interiors. It describes the outcome without inventing a price, duration or technical guarantee.

### Option B
**BM:** Bina rumah atas tanah sendiri dengan langkah yang lebih jelas.
**EN:** Build on your own land with a clearer path forward.

**Rationale:** This speaks directly to the strongest qualified audience identified in the brief: people who already have land and need a residential construction conversation. “Lebih jelas / clearer” is a process position, not a claim of fixed pricing or guaranteed delivery.

### Option C
**BM:** Dari pelan rumah ke ruang yang sudah siap didiami.
**EN:** From a house plan to a finished living space.

**Rationale:** The supplied portfolio includes exterior houses and finished interior rooms. Use this only if Binali confirms it commonly builds from client or professional plans; otherwise change “pelan rumah” to “idea rumah”.

**Recommended hero subheadline BM:** Binali Construction & Hardware membantu pemilik tanah membincangkan pembinaan rumah kediaman setingkat moden, termasuk tenaga kerja dan bahan binaan mengikut skop projek yang dipersetujui.

### The three numbers that matter

1. **Qualified enquiry rate:** qualified enquiries divided by landing-page sessions. This measures whether traffic turns into viable projects, not merely clicks.
2. **Cost per qualified enquiry by source:** ad spend divided by qualified enquiries, split into Facebook, TikTok and organic. This tells you where to spend.
3. **Qualified enquiry to site-visit or quotation rate:** the share of qualified enquiries that reach the next real sales step. This catches weak follow-up and poor lead quality.

Raw page views, likes and WhatsApp clicks are secondary diagnostics. They are not the outcome.
